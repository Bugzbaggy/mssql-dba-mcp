## A note on the published version

This project began as internal DBA tooling. The public version differs from
the internal one in two ways:

1. **Identifiers replaced.** Hostnames, database names, service accounts, IP
   addresses, and internal DNS suffixes are placeholders throughout.
2. **Fleet-specific content removed.** The bundled schema documentation for
   internal databases, the internal CA bundle, and the runbooks describing
   one particular environment's quirks and migration state are not included.

No server logic was changed.
